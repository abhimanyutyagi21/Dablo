A video with 8,000 views can earn more than one with 80,000. Put attributed revenue beside the content, then investigate the hook, offer, audience, and next step. The numbers show where to look, not a guaranteed reason why.

Questions, answered

Straight answers.
No pretend certainty.

Dablo is in development. These answers describe the intended product and what the first version can reasonably support.

What is Dablo?

Dablo is a revenue tracking and analytics platform for people who use content to grow an audience, business, or income. It brings revenue, expenses, content, tracking links, and campaigns into one workspace.

Who is Dablo for?

Creators, founders, personal-brand builders, affiliate marketers, consultants, and independent professionals who earn through content.

Can I track revenue from an individual video or post?

Yes, by associating a revenue record with the relevant content manually or through reliable conversion data from a supported integration. Views alone do not prove that a piece of content generated a sale.

How do tracking links work?

A unique link can be assigned to a video, post, product, or campaign. A deployed redirect endpoint is required to record clicks. Purchases require conversion data from a supported service or manual entry.

Are clicks the same as sales?

No. Clicks show that someone opened a link. They do not confirm a purchase. Dablo should show conversion and revenue only when the data is available and the attribution is justified.

Can I track sponsorships and affiliate income?

The intended workflow supports recording sponsorship deals, deliverables, payment status, campaign expenses, affiliate commissions, and related content. Automatic imports depend on future supported integrations.

Can Dablo track expenses and profit?

Yes. The planned dashboard calculates net profit as total revenue minus total expenses, using the records available in your account.

What does “Why It Converted” do?

It helps you compare content and record possible explanations, such as the hook, audience intent, product fit, offer, and call to action. These are hypotheses unless the available evidence supports a stronger conclusion.

What are Content Success Frameworks?

They are editable checklists you save from patterns worth testing again. Each framework can link back to the content that inspired it. They are a learning tool, not a guarantee of results.

Does Dablo connect to every platform automatically?

No. The initial version relies on manual entry. Integrations and automatic imports can be added where platforms provide suitable access and permissions.

How will financial data be protected?

The product plan includes authenticated accounts, database access controls, and limited collection of tracking information. The cloud implementation and security policies must be in place before these protections can be considered live.

Can I use Dablo today?

Not yet. Dablo is in development. Join the early-access list to hear about availability. This form is a prototype and does not send your email to a backend yet.
